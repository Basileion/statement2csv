QuickBooks Online bank CSV format

Per Intuit's help articles:

  • Each upload file needs either 3 columns (Date, Description, Amount) or 4 columns (Date, Description, Credit, Debit).
  • In the 3-column layout, payments are negative and deposits positive.
  • The upload file must be 350 KB or less.
  • Leave zero-only amount cells blank, remove numbers from the Description column, and keep every date in one format (Intuit recommends dd/mm/yyyy).

Sources: Manually upload transactions into QuickBooks Online; Format CSV files in Excel.

Convert a PDF statement to that format

Choose "QuickBooks 3-column" in the converter. It writes signed amounts with no currency symbols or thousands separators and strips digits from descriptions.

Choose a PDF. Nothing is uploaded: parsing runs on this page.

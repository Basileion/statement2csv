Privacy

  • The converter is a static page. Your PDF is read with Mozilla pdf.js (Apache-2.0, served from this site) inside your browser tab.
  • No file, row or amount is sent to any server. There is no account and no analytics script on this site.
  • You can verify this by opening your browser's network tab while converting, or by loading the page and then going offline.

Statement2CSV converts text-based bank and card statement PDFs to CSV in your browser using Mozilla pdf.js. Files never leave your device. Scanned (image) PDFs are not supported. Always check totals against the statement.

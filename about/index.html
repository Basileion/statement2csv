Methodology

How it works

  1. pdf.js reads the PDF's text layer in your browser.
  2. Text is regrouped into lines by position. A line that starts with a date and ends with money amounts becomes a transaction; short lines below it are joined to its description.
  3. When a running balance is printed, each amount's sign is set from the change in balance, so withdrawals come out negative.
  4. Opening and previous-balance lines are dropped.

Limits

  • Scanned or photographed statements have no text layer and are not supported (no OCR).
  • Layouts vary by bank. Check the row count and the money-in / money-out totals against your statement before importing.
  • Without a balance column, unsigned amounts stay positive; use Flip signs or fix in your spreadsheet.

Statement2CSV converts text-based bank and card statement PDFs to CSV in your browser using Mozilla pdf.js. Files never leave your device. Scanned (image) PDFs are not supported. Always check totals against the statement.
